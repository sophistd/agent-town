## Questions

Within 10 seconds, a reviewer should answer at least three of four:

- [ ] Who is the active agent?
- [ ] What phase is the current task in?
- [ ] Is there a blocked or error state?
- [ ] Who was involved in the most recent handoff or message?

## Visual Evidence

- [ ] 7 locations are visible: Town Hall, Library, Workshop, Archive, Review
      Room, Dispatch Board, Square.
- [ ] 5 default agents are visible: Planner, Researcher, Coder, Reviewer,
      Memory.
- [ ] Active, waiting, blocked, error, and done states are visually distinct.
- [ ] Handoff or message edge is visible when the fixture reaches one.
- [ ] Tool location is recognizable from agent position.
- [ ] Bubble or marker links to the underlying event through Detail Panel.
